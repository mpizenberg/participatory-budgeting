Ranking > Rating to force people to take decisions, mitigate max-raters, reveals what truly matters.

This ref on ranking methods is quite good: https://www.opinionx.co/blog/choosing-a-survey-ranking-method

Approaches:
- Direct ranking of all options
- Points allocation
- Pairwise comparisons
- MaxDiff (best-worst of subsets of 3-6 items)
- Conjoint analysis (e.g. brand x price x battery)

When choosing a single winner: studies show IRV and Condorcet give best results
- First-Past-the-Post (count only first choices)
- Instant-Runoff Voting (IRV, iterative eliminate lowest and reallocate ballots until one > 50%)
- Borda Count (descending points to ranks)
- Condorcet Methods: (best in pairwise contests)
